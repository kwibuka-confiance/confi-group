# User Personas

## Business Owner

Needs:

- Overall business visibility
- Profit and cash insight
- User and permission control
- Subscription management
- Multi-branch reporting

## Branch Manager

Needs:

- Daily operations visibility
- Inventory approvals
- Discount approvals
- Branch-level reports
- Staff oversight

## Cashier

Needs:

- Fast product search
- Reliable checkout
- Clear payment status
- Receipt printing
- Simple return workflow

## Warehouse Staff

Needs:

- Receiving
- Transfers
- Stock counts
- Damage recording
- Barcode scanning

## Sales Representative

Needs:

- Customer history
- Wholesale orders
- Quotations
- Outstanding balances
- Customer follow-up

## Driver

Needs:

- Assigned deliveries
- Navigation
- Delivery status
- Proof of delivery
- Collection confirmation

## Accountant

Needs:

- Expenses
- Reconciliation
- Revenue reports
- Payment records
- Audit trails

## Platform Administrator

Needs:

- Tenant provisioning
- Platform health
- Feature rollout
- Billing support
- Security oversight
