# Business Model

## Purpose

Define how Confi Group creates, delivers, and captures value.

## Business 1: KwaConfi Depot

KwaConfi Depot generates cash flow through:

- Beverage wholesale
- Beverage retail
- Small bar sales
- Billiards
- Soft drinks, water, and snacks
- Delivery fees

Its strategic purpose is also to validate ConfiOS in daily operations.

## Business 2: ConfiOS SaaS

ConfiOS generates recurring revenue through subscriptions.

## Go-to-Market Strategy

1. Validate at KwaConfi Depot.
2. Onboard a small number of nearby depots.
3. Improve based on operational feedback.
4. Add subscription billing.
5. Introduce customer ordering and WhatsApp.
6. Expand geographically.
