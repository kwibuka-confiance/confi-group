# Brand Guidelines

## Parent Company

**Confi Group Ltd**

## Business Units

- KwaConfi Depot

- ConfiOS
- Confi AI

## Brand Personality

- Trustworthy
- Modern
- Practical
- African
- Intelligent
- Approachable

## Voice

Use language that is:

- Clear
- Direct
- Respectful
- Professional
- Easy to understand

Avoid unnecessary technical jargon in customer-facing communication.

## Tagline Direction

Preferred:

> Driven by Innovation, Built on Trust.

Alternative:

> Smart Solutions. Real Growth.
