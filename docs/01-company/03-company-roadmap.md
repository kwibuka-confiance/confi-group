# Company Roadmap

## Phase 1: Foundation

- Register and organize Confi Group Ltd
- Launch KwaConfi Depot
- Build internal ConfiOS MVP
- Establish operating procedures
- Measure real business workflows

## Phase 2: Operational Product

- Complete inventory, sales, purchases, customers, suppliers, and reporting
- Improve reliability
- Create onboarding and support processes
- Add multilingual support

## Phase 3: SaaS Pilot

- Multi-tenant onboarding
- Subscription plans
- Feature licensing
- Pilot with selected depots
- Gather structured feedback

## Phase 4: Commerce Network

- Customer ordering app
- Driver app
- WhatsApp ordering
- Payment integrations
- Delivery tracking

## Phase 5: Intelligence

- AI assistant
- Demand forecasting
- Reorder recommendations
- Customer churn signals
- Automated business summaries

## Phase 6: Regional Expansion

- Multi-country configuration
- Additional currencies
- Additional languages
- Country-specific tax support
- Expansion into other industries
