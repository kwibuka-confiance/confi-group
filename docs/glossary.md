# Glossary

## Tenant

An independent business using ConfiOS.

## Branch

A business location belonging to a tenant.

## Warehouse

A stock-holding location belonging to a branch.

## Stock Movement

An immutable record of inventory change.

## Receive Stock

Increase inventory because goods arrived.

## Adjust Stock

Correct inventory based on an authorized reason.

## Transfer Stock

Move inventory between warehouses.

## Archive

Hide a business record from normal use without destroying history.

## Domain Event

An immutable record that a meaningful business action occurred.

## Feature Flag

A configuration that enables or disables a platform capability.
